## Missing evidence is marked missing

The documents do not supply a favorable single-context CPU budget, source/sink contract, and profile-matched advantage for this method. EyeSat's allocated compression-partition RAM, PROBA-V's exact flight bus ordering/arbitration, and LandIS's final source/DMA scheduling are not established. No values were synthesized for them. HYPSO's complete-cube processing and reserved input/output memory are directly documented.

`literature/retrievals.json` preserves successful downloads and failures. Third-party documents remain research materials, not our authored scientific results. Source locations and caveats in this ledger and the final report are the evidential record where a full local download was unavailable.
